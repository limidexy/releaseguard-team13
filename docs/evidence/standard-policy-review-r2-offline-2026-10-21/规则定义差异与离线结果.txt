STANDARD_POLICY 评审定义修订 r2（仅离线）

旧定义：State that standard returns require an unused item within 30 days of purchase with a receipt.
新定义：Pass only if the answer correctly expresses all three jointly necessary standard-return conditions (unused item, no more than 30 days from purchase, and a receipt) AND the entire answer contains no contrary policy assertion made or endorsed by the answerer. A later cancellation, relaxation or denial of any condition makes STANDARD_POLICY failed, even after a correct policy statement. EXTRA_CLAIMS_AUTHORIZED may also fail but cannot substitute for this rule. Distinguish the answerer's assertions from attributed customer quotations and explicitly rejected/corrected erroneous examples.

通过必须同时满足：正确表达未使用、购买后不超过30天、持有收据三个共同必要条件；全文不存在回答者自己作出的相反政策断言。
后文取消、放宽或否定任一条件时，STANDARD_POLICY 必须 failed。EXTRA_CLAIMS_AUTHORIZED 可同时失败，不能替代这一逐规则结论。
区分回答者的政策断言、仅归属于客户的引用、已明确否定并纠正的错误示例。否定句或引用本身不构成违规；引述若被回答者采纳或重申仍属回答者断言。归属或纠正范围无法可靠判断时保持 unverifiable，不强行通过。

Slide 6
Presenter: B · ZHU PENGXU · Target: 1.5 minutes

Script:
Two decisions shaped the logical architecture. First, we split by bounded context into three business services instead of a modular monolith or six fine-grained services: a monolith cannot scale execution, and six services would add distributed transactions without a business reason. Second, evaluation runs asynchronously through a durable queue: the API accepts the job, commits it with an outbox entry in one transaction, and competing workers consume it. This is Queue-Based Load Leveling with Competing Consumers; the cost is at-least-once delivery, which we handle with idempotent receivers. The other four decisions are summarised below.
